MitiBar keeps today's BS date in the tray, with a month grid, tithi, public holidays, a converter and
settings one click away. On Windows a small floating bar shows the date and time wherever you put it.
The popup opens from the tray, the bar or a global shortcut, Win+Shift+N, and closes when it loses
focus. It makes no network requests.

It is built on the [Rust library](/docs/rust/) and lives in its own repository,
[sanamhub/mitibar](https://github.com/sanamhub/mitibar), with its own version line.

## What it does

- **Tray**: today's BS day on the icon on Windows and Linux, and the bar's top line as the menu-bar
  title on macOS. The tooltip has the BS and AD dates. The menu has Open, Copy today (BS), Copy
  today (AD), Show bar (Windows), Settings and Quit.
- **Floating bar (Windows)**: one or two lines you choose from the weekday, the BS day, month and
  year, the BS and AD dates, the time, a second clock such as `SGT 10:11 AM`, tithi and today's
  holiday, each line bold or regular. It starts with the weekday and AD date over the BS date in
  bold; the Default, Date and time, Minimal and All presets set both lines at once, and one button
  swaps them.
  Drag it anywhere; it snaps to edges, stays above the taskbar and hides while a full-screen app is
  in front.
- **Month view**: BS and AD day numbers, tithi under each day, holidays in red, events marked with a
  dot, and today as a tile. Zoom out to a year or a decade, go to any date, or pin the popup open.
- **Converter**: BS to AD, AD to BS, and the days between two dates, with a Copy button for each
  result.
- **Settings**: language (English or Nepali), theme, the bar's two lines, the shortcut, a second
  time zone, and start at login.

## Build it yourself

```sh
git clone https://github.com/sanamhub/mitibar
cd mitibar
cargo run --release
```

On Linux it needs a tray (StatusNotifierItem or the GNOME AppIndicator extension), fontconfig,
xkbcommon and D-Bus. The renderer is software only, so no OpenGL or GPU driver is needed.
