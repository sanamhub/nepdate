---
title: Parsing
description: The strict and lenient parsers every port has, and what each accepts.
---

Every port has two parsers. Both reject rather than guess, and both give the same answer in every
language for the same input. The normative text is
[PARSING.md](https://github.com/sanamhub/nepdate/blob/main/spec/PARSING.md) in the hub, and every
port runs its `parse.tsv` vectors.

## Strict

Exactly **year, month, day**, in that order.

- Digits are ASCII `0` to `9` or Devanagari `०` to `९`, and may be mixed.
- Separators are `-`, `/`, `.`, `_`, `\`, a space, `।` or `|`. Leading, trailing and repeated
  separators are ignored.
- Anything else is an error, as is a fourth group or a group longer than four digits.

All of these are BS 2080-05-15:

```
2080/05/15
2080-5-15
  2080 / 05 / 15
/2080/05/15/
2080।05।15
२०८०/०५/१५
```

## Lenient

For what a person types into a form. It tries the strict parser first, then:

- A **month name** with two numbers: the four-digit number is the year and the other is the day,
  in any order. `15 Shrawan 2080`, `Shrawan 15, 2080` and `2080 Shrawan 15` are the same date.
- **Three numbers**: `Y M D` when the first has four digits, `D M Y` when the last does.
- Commas count as separators. Era markers (`BS`, `B.S.`, `VS`, <span lang="ne">`वि.सं.`</span> and
  their variants) and the words <span lang="ne">`गते`</span> and <span lang="ne">`मिति`</span> are
  dropped.
- Month names are matched whole and without regard to case for Latin letters, from a closed list
  of English spellings and the Nepali names.

It refuses to guess: a two-digit year is never expanded, and input such as `05/06/07` is
**ambiguous**, an error, rather than one of its three readings.

## Errors

Both parsers report a kind your code can act on:

| Kind | When |
|---|---|
| Invalid character | strict: a character that is neither a digit nor a separator |
| Wrong group count | strict: not exactly three numbers |
| Number too long | a number with more than four digits |
| Ambiguous | lenient: no way to tell the year from the day |
| Unrecognized | lenient: an unknown word, two month names, or nothing at all |
| Out of range, invalid month, invalid day | the numbers parse but aren't a BS date between 1901 and 2199 |

Each language spells these in its own style, for example `ParseErrorKind::Ambiguous` in Rust or
`"ambiguous"` in TypeScript.
