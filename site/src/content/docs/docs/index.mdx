NepDate is the Bikram Sambat calendar (BS, the official calendar of Nepal) as a family of libraries
that behave the same in every language, and a few apps built on them.

Every library converts between BS and AD for **BS 1901-01-01 to 2199-12-30** (AD 1844-04-11 to
2143-04-15), formats and parses Nepali dates in English and Nepali, does date arithmetic and fiscal
years, and gives the same answer as every other port. The answers are checked against the same test
vectors in each repository.

## The libraries

Each language ships two packages with one version, now <code>{VERSION}</code>, a public pre-release:

- **The core**: conversion, arithmetic, fiscal year, formatting and parsing. No dependencies and no
  almanac data.
- **The patro** (पात्रो, the Nepali almanac): tithi, public holidays and events for BS 2077 to 2084.
  It depends on the core, never the other way round.

The C# original, [NepDate](https://github.com/RajuPrasai/NepDate) by Raju Prasai, is where the
calendar data and the algorithm come from. It ships as one package with its own versions. The ports
match it, and where they differ on purpose, they all differ the same way
([Same answer everywhere](/docs/project/conformance/)).

Every port is also faster than every other Bikram Sambat library we could measure in its language
([Performance](/docs/project/performance/)).

## The apps

- **[MitiBar](/docs/apps/mitibar/)**: a tray calendar for Windows, macOS and Linux with a floating
  date bar on Windows, built on the Rust library.
- **[NepDate for Android and iOS](/docs/apps/mobile/)**: a month view, holidays and home-screen
  widgets, in development.
